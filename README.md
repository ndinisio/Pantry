# Pantry

**Know what you have. Know what to cook. Know what to buy.**

Pantry is a native iOS 26 app for keeping track of the food in your kitchen and turning
that inventory into meals. You add what you own; Pantry suggests what to cook, warns you
about what needs using, and works out what is worth buying.

Work in progress — see `CHANGELOG.md` for what has landed so far.
